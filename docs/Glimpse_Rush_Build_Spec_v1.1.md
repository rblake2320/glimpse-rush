## 1. Product summary

Glimpse Rush is a mobile photo-reveal guessing game. A photo is hidden under a grid of tiles. Tiles flip on a timer. The player taps **Freeze** when they think they know the photo, then has 7 seconds to type the answer. Fewer tiles revealed + more time left = more points.

**Tagline:** Same photo. Same clock. See it first.

**Platforms:** iOS + Android (single codebase).

## 3. World Live — the 60-second cycle

All times are server time, measured from `cycle_start` (aligned to wall-clock :00 of every minute UTC).

| Phase | Offset (ms) | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| JOIN | 0 – 4,999 | Lobby shows countdown and player count. Photo, grid size, category show `??`. |
| REVEAL | 5,000 – 34,999 | Tiles flip on shared server clock (Section 4). Freeze allowed. |
| FINAL_WINDOW | 35,000 – 44,999 | All tiles visible. Freeze allowed; flat low score (Section 5.4). |
| RESULTS | 45,000 – 59,999 | Answer + full photo shown. Round leaderboard. "Next round in 0:xx". |

Rules:
- `reveal_start = cycle_start + 5,000 ms`. `REVEAL_MS = 30,000`.
- Players may join mid-round; client syncs to current server state (tiles already revealed are shown). No score advantage for late joiners.
- A player is in at most one World Live round at a time.

## 4. Tile reveal algorithm

- Grid `N` ∈ {4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 64, 100} (World Live uses only 25/36/64/100). Layout: square √N × √N.
- Reveal order: a permutation of 0..N-1 generated by the server from a secret per-round seed (Fisher–Yates). **Same order for every player in a round.** Seed is never sent to clients.
- Tile 1 is visible at `reveal_start` (elapsed = 0). Tile *k* becomes visible at `elapsed = (k-1) × REVEAL_MS / N`.
- **Integer formula (use this, no floats):**
  `tiles_shown(elapsed_ms) = min(N, floor(elapsed_ms × N / 30000) + 1)`
- Interval examples: 25 → 1,200 ms, 36 → 833.3 ms, 64 → 468.75 ms, 100 → 300 ms.

### 4a. Scramble levels (v1.1)

An optional difficulty setting that shuffles where each piece of the photo sits on the board.

| Level | Board | Multiplier S |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Off | Normal photo | 1.0 |
| 1 Scrambled | Grid cell *i* shows source piece `src[i]` (a permutation, never the identity) | 1.25 |
| 2 Scrambled + turned | Level 1, and each piece is also rotated `turn[i] × 90°` | 1.5 |

- `src` and `turn` come from a **separate scramble seed** (Fisher–Yates for `src`, uniform 0–3 for `turn`). The reveal order (Section 4) still runs over **cells**, so timing and `tiles_shown` are unchanged.
- **Same for everyone** (default): scramble seed = round seed. **Different per player** (Group Room and Practice only): scramble seed derived per player, so each person sees a different arrangement of the same photo.
- The server pre-cuts and pre-rotates the piece for each cell, so the client never learns a piece's source position.
- RESULTS always shows the unscrambled photo.
- **Where it's offered in v1:** Solo Practice and Group Room (host setting), and **World Live: Scrambled** (v1.1.3), a separate ranked queue where every round is level 2, same for everyone, scored on its own boards (`lb_world_scr_day`, `lb_world_scr_week`). Regular World Live, Daily, and Blitz stay at level 0, so their boards remain comparable.

## 5. Scoring

### 5.1 Formula (World Live, Daily, Blitz)

> **Canonical implementation (v1.1):** compute in integers/rationals, apply **all** multipliers (G, H, T, P, partial, hint) **before** a single final rounding, rounding **half-up** (x.5 → up). Do not use banker's rounding (Python `round`, C# default) and do not round intermediate values. The float form below is explanatory only.
>
> ```
> P4 = 4 (first freeze) | 3 (second freeze)
> M  = 1 normally; 2 if partial; 2 if hint (multiply: partial+hint = 4)
> num = G100 × (N − tiles_shown) × (30000 + 3 × time_left_ms) × P4
> den = 24000 × N × M                   # G100 = G × 100 (integer: 50,75,90,100,150,200,300)
> raw = (2 × num + den) div (2 × den)   # integer half-up
> ```

```
Score = round( 1000 × G × H × T × P )
H = (N − tiles_shown) / N                 # hidden fraction, uses tiles shown at freeze moment
T = 0.5 + 1.5 × time_left_ms / 30000      # 0.5 … 2.0
time_left_ms = max(0, 30000 − elapsed_ms) # elapsed measured at server freeze timestamp, millisecond precision
P = 1.0 first freeze, 0.75 second freeze  # freeze penalty
S = 1.0 | 1.25 | 1.5                      # scramble level (Section 4a); also multiplies the 5.4 flat score
```

Integer form with scramble: `num = G100 × (N − s) × (30000 + 3 × time_left_ms) × P4 × S4`, `den = 96000 × N × M`, where `S4` = 4 / 5 / 6 for levels 0 / 1 / 2.

### 5.2 Grid multiplier G

| N | 4 | 9 | 16 | 25 | 36 | 64 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G | 0.5 | 0.75 | 0.9 | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | 3.0 |

(4/9/16 are Solo Practice / Group Room only.)

### 5.3 Hint multiplier (Solo Practice and Group Room only)
`Score × 0.5` when hint is on. Never available in ranked modes; UI must disable "Show Hint" when Ranked is selected.

### 5.4 Final answer window
Correct answer submitted from a freeze during FINAL_WINDOW: flat `50 × G × P` points, with partial/hint multipliers applied, single half-up rounding (same rule as 5.1).

### 5.4a Late-REVEAL floor (v1.1)
Without a floor, a correct freeze in the last ~2.5 s of REVEAL scores **less** than waiting for FINAL_WINDOW (N=36: freeze at 28,000 ms = 50 pts vs. 75 flat). Rule: a correct REVEAL score is `max(formula_score, final_window_score)` computed with the same P/partial/hint. This removes the incentive to wait.

### 5.5 Partial answers
Each photo may define partial answers (e.g. "dog" for Golden Retriever). Partial = `Score × 0.5`.

### 5.6 Maximum possible scores (for validation; reject anything higher)
(Bound is P=1, no partial/hint; the 5.4a floor never exceeds it.)
`max = round(1000 × G × (N−1)/N × 2.0)` → 25: 1,920 · 36: 2,917 · 64: 3,938 · 100: 5,940. With scramble, multiply the bound by S before rounding (N=100 at level 2: 8,910).

### 5.7 Ties
Rank by score desc, then by `freeze_server_ts` asc, then by `answer_submit_ts` asc.

## 6. Freeze & answer flow

1. Player taps **Freeze**. Client sends `freeze` event. **Server** stamps `freeze_server_ts` (client time is never trusted).
2. Server replies `freeze_ack { tiles_shown, time_left_ms, answer_deadline }`. Player's board stops at `tiles_shown`. World continues.
3. Answer window: **7,000 ms** + 500 ms server grace for latency.
4. Outcomes:
   - Correct → score locked using values from step 1.
   - **Partial (v1.1.1):** in World Live (ranked) a partial answer locks the score like a correct one. In Solo Practice, Solo Blitz and Group Room, a partial answer on the **first** freeze banks its score and the round keeps running; the player may use the second freeze (P = 0.75) to try for the full answer. The round scores the **higher** of the banked partial and the second-freeze result; a wrong answer, timeout, Unfreeze or Give up on the second freeze keeps the banked partial. A partial on the second freeze ends the round as usual.
   - Wrong, timeout, or **Unfreeze** tapped → board jumps to live state; freezes remaining decrements. No score.
5. **Max 2 freezes per round.** Second freeze uses P = 0.75. After the second failure, player is out for the round (spectates).
6. Unfreeze button label: "Unfreeze — 1 freeze left" (or "— last freeze" on the second).
7. Answer input: single text field; autocomplete from a general noun dictionary (≥5,000 words), **never** from the round's answer list.

## 7. Answer matching (server-side)

1. Normalize: lowercase, trim, collapse whitespace, strip punctuation, strip leading "a/an/the", singularize simple plurals.
2. Compare against `accepted[]` (full credit) then `partial[]` (half credit).
3. Fuzzy tolerance (v1.1): **exact only** for normalized length ≤ 3; Levenshtein ≤ 1 for length 4–6; ≤ 2 for length > 6. Never fuzzy-match across two different accepted entries.
3a. **Real-word guard:** if the normalized input is itself a word in the dictionary (Section 6.7) and is not an exact accepted/partial entry, do not fuzzy-match it. Prevents "hose" → "horse", "car" → "cat", "fog" → "dog".
3b. Fuzzy applies to partial entries under the same limits.
4. Each photo record must have ≥1 accepted answer and an optional partial list. Content team curates; no AI auto-acceptance in v1.

## 8. Anti-cheat

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Server authority | Round clock, tile order, freeze timestamps, scoring, matching — all server-side. |
| Tile delivery | Photo is pre-cut into N tiles. Each tile is delivered only at its reveal time (WebSocket push of short-lived signed URL, TTL 60 s). Full image is never sent before RESULTS. |
| No pre-reveal metadata | Photo, grid, category hidden until `reveal_start`. |
| App backgrounding | In ranked modes, if app loses focus while frozen → freeze fails (counts as wrong). |
| Screenshots | Detect (iOS `userDidTakeScreenshotNotification`, Android 14+ screenshot callback). Flag score; 3 flags/week → review. |
| Score bounds | Reject scores > Section 5.6 maximum or with freeze_ts outside REVEAL/FINAL_WINDOW. |
| Daily answer leakage | Daily set is one attempt per photo; results/answers hidden until the player finishes all 5. |
| Rate limits | 1 freeze event per 300 ms per player; max 2 freezes per round enforced server-side. |

## 9. Leaderboards

**Scopes:** Global · Friends · each Group. **Periods:** This Round (World Live) · Today · This Week.

| Board | Ranking metric |
|---|---|
| This Round | Round score (Section 5.7 ties) |
| Today | Sum of player's best 10 World Live round scores today |
| This Week | Sum of player's best 20 World Live round scores this week (prevents grinding) |
| Daily Challenge | Sum of the 5 daily photo scores |
| Solo Blitz | Best single Blitz run this week |
| Secondary weekly boards | Fastest correct freeze (lowest elapsed_ms); Lowest % revealed |

**Key derivation (v1.1):** `day_key` and `week_key` are computed from the **America/Chicago local timestamp** of `freeze_server_ts`, not UTC. `week_key` uses the ISO week-year (Dec 29–Jan 3 can belong to the adjacent year). DST transitions (2026-11-01, 2027-03-14) must not shift a period boundary.

**Resets:** Today at 00:00 America/Chicago. This Week at **Monday 00:00 America/Chicago**. Do not delete history — tag every score with `day_key` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `week_key` (ISO, e.g. 2026-W40); old periods remain viewable.

Implementation: Redis sorted sets per (board, scope, period) for live ranks; Postgres is the system of record.

## 10. Other modes — rules

**Daily Challenge:** 5 photos, N=36, World-Live timing for each photo (5 s countdown, 30 s reveal, 10 s final window). One attempt per photo, 2 freezes per photo. Same set for all players worldwide that day.

**Solo Blitz:** 120,000 ms total run clock. Photos at N=25 with 30 s reveal each; next photo starts immediately after an answer or Skip. Run clock **pauses** during a freeze answer window (max 7 s each). Run score = sum of photo scores. Skip = 0 for that photo, no other penalty. **(v1.1)** The photo's own reveal clock pauses with the run clock during a freeze. After every answer, skip, or two failed freezes, the full photo and points show for 1.3 s with the run clock paused, then the next photo starts. After a photo's 30 s reveal, all tiles stay up (final-window flat score) until answer, skip, or run end. Run ends at 0:00 or **End run**.

**Solo Practice:** Any grid, hint optional, no leaderboard, unlimited plays. Uses a separate practice photo pool (never ranked photos). **Early end (v1.1, amended v1.1.1):** a correct answer ends the round immediately (a partial answer on the first freeze is banked and the round continues; see §6 step 4) and reveals the full photo with the score breakdown; so does using both freezes, or tapping **Give up & reveal** (scores 0). Synced modes (World Live, Daily, Group Room) still run the full clock.

**Group Room:** Any user (parent, friend, team lead, company admin) creates a private room → 6-char code (A–Z minus I/L/O, 2–9) + invite link (deep link). Host settings: grid (any single size, or **Mixed** = server picks 25/36/64/100 per round), rounds (5 or 10), hint on/off, start now or schedule. Max 50 players. Synced clock like World Live; results feed that group's board only. **(v1.1)** Each round shows a room board (this round + running total); the game ends with final standings and the host can start a new game in the same room. If the host disconnects, any member can take over as host. Players can join mid-round (no score advantage) or mid-game (earlier rounds count as 0).

### 10a. Custom photo packs (v1.1)

Any user can build a **private photo pack** and use it in Solo Practice or as the photo source for a Group Room (family reunion, friend group, team, company, dealership). Packs never enter World Live, Daily, or Blitz, and pack scores never reach global boards.

**Upload.** JPEG/PNG/HEIC/WebP, ≤ 15 MB each, ≤ 200 photos per pack. Server strips EXIF/GPS, center-crops to square (host can adjust crop), resizes to 1080 px, and pre-cuts tiles for every grid size (Section 8 tile delivery applies unchanged).

**Answer rules, set by the host per photo:**

| Field | Required | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Correct answers | ≥ 1 | Full credit. Multiple entries for spellings, nicknames, short forms ("Porsche 911 GT3", "911 GT3"; "Uncle Jim Bob", "Jim Bob") |
| Half credit | Optional | `Score × 0.5` (e.g. "Porsche" when the model is expected; "engine" when "V8 engine" is expected) |
| Not accepted | Optional | Never matches, even within typo distance; checked **first** ("car", "sports car" at a dealership) |
| Exact spelling | Toggle, default off | Off: Section 7 typo tolerance applies. On: normalized exact match only |
| Hint | Optional | Shown only when the room's hint setting is on (×0.5) |

**Matching order for pack photos:** normalize (Section 7.1, plus hyphen/underscore/slash/period → space, then compare with spaces removed so "Jim-Bob" = "jim bob" = "jimbob") → not-accepted → correct → half credit → (if exact spelling off) real-word guard → typo tolerance, where a guess within tolerance of a not-accepted entry fails.

**Suggested answers.** The host may request AI suggestions per photo (alternate spellings/nicknames for full credit, broader terms for half credit, too-generic terms to block). Suggestions appear as chips; nothing is added until the host taps it. For photos of people, the assistant only varies the names the host typed and never identifies anyone from the image. No AI auto-acceptance (Section 7.4 holds).

**Answers stay server-side.** Pack answers are never sent to player devices; the server judges every guess, as in Section 7. *(The playtest sends each round's answers to players' devices; this is a preview shortcut, not v1 behavior.)*

**People in photos.** Pack visibility is private to the group. The uploader confirms they have permission to share each photo with the group. Photos of people, including minors in family packs, can never be published beyond the group. No face recognition or identification is run. Deleting a photo or pack removes the original and all tiles within 24 hours and drops it from future rounds; past scores remain.

**Safety (all uploads, private or public).** Hash-match against known child sexual abuse material lists before storage (e.g. PhotoDNA / NCMEC hash sets), with reporting under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A; classifier block on nudity and graphic violence; in-room **Report photo** button; repeat violators lose upload rights.

**Public challenge packs (post-MVP, design only).** A pack can be submitted for others to play only if the uploader attests each photo is their own or licensed for this use, and gives source + license per photo (Section 16). Public packs exclude identifiable private people, enter a moderation queue, and are covered by a registered DMCA agent and takedown flow (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)). Public packs get their own boards, never the ranked World Live pool.

**Data model additions:** `packs(id, owner_id, name, visibility['private'|'public_pending'|'public'|'rejected'], created_at)` · `pack_photos(pack_id, photo_id, position, strict, hint, uploader_attested_at)` · `photo_answers.kind` adds `'blocked'` · `photos.pool` adds `'pack'` · `rooms.photo_source('builtin'|pack_id)`.

**Group Room host settings** gain **Photos: Built-in | a pack** and **Scramble: Off | Scrambled | Scrambled + turned**, with **Different scramble for each player** on or off. If the pack has fewer ready photos than rounds, photos repeat in order.

**Acceptance tests (add to Section 18):**
9. Pack photo with correct "Porsche 911 GT3, 911 GT3", half "Porsche", not accepted "car": "911 gt-3" → full; "porsche" → half; "car" and "cars" → 0.
10. Exact spelling on, correct "Uncle Jim Bob": "uncle jim-bob" → full; "uncle jim bobb" → 0.
11. Network capture during a pack round contains no answer text before RESULTS.
12. A pack containing a person photo cannot be submitted as a public pack.

**Groups (persistent):** Users create named groups (family, friends, social media group), invite by link; group boards show members' World Live / Daily / Blitz scores.

## 18. Acceptance tests (minimum)

1. Two clients in the same World Live round receive identical tile order and reveal timestamps (±50 ms).
2. Client with manipulated clock cannot change its score.
3. Network capture during REVEAL contains no full-resolution image and no unrevealed tile URLs.
4. Third freeze attempt in a round is rejected.
5. Answer "golden retriver" (typo) matches "golden retriever"; "dog" scores partial; "labrador" fails.
6. Backgrounding the app while frozen in ranked mode = failed freeze.
7. Weekly board for 2026-W40 remains readable after reset to 2026-W41.
8. Any score above Section 5.6 maximum is rejected and logged.

## 20. Profiles, teams, and live leaderboards (v1.1)

### 20.1 Profiles
- **Sign-in:** Apple, Google, or email (Firebase Auth). A player can start as a **guest** on one device; signing in later moves that device's scores to the account (one-time merge, server-side, logged).
- **Profile fields:** handle (unique, 3–20 letters/numbers/underscore, case-insensitive), display name, avatar (uploaded image, goes through the §10a safety pipeline), country (optional), settings (sound, haptics, default grid, default scramble).
- **Profile page:** best World Live round, fastest correct freeze, lowest % revealed, Blitz best, Daily streak, achievements, groups and current teams.
- **Age:** accounts are 13+ (age gate at sign-up). Under-13 players can only join a Group Room as a guest created by an adult account holder; no profile, no public boards.
- **Account deletion** in-app (App Store and Google Play requirement): profile PII scrubbed within 30 days; scores stay in the ledger under the opaque user id and show as "Deleted player".

### 20.2 Teams
- **Room teams (ad hoc):** host turns on Teams (2–4) for one game; players pick a team in the lobby. Default names and colors Red / Blue / Green / Gold; host can rename.
- **Persistent teams:** owners/admins of a group create named teams (e.g. "Sales" vs "Service" at a dealership, "Blake side" vs "Johnson side" at a reunion). Membership history is kept; a player is on at most one active team per group. Rooms started from a group can use its persistent teams.
- **Team scoring:** a team's score for a round = **average** of its players' round scores (a player with no correct answer counts 0), integer half-up. Team game total = sum of its round scores. Averaging keeps a team of 3 competitive with a team of 8. Ties: higher number of correct answers, then earliest last correct freeze.
- **Joining mid-game:** counts from the next round; earlier rounds are 0 for that player and do not change the team's earlier round scores.

### 20.3 Live leaderboards
Boards update while a round is still running, not only at RESULTS.

| Board | Scope | Live updates |
|---|---|---|
| This round (World Live) | Global · Friends · Group | Each correct answer, within 1 s |
| Room (Group Room) | Players and teams in the room | Each freeze/answer: status chips (answering, locked in, missed, out) and running team totals |
| Today / This Week | Global · Friends · Group · Country | When a round's results post |
| Team week | Persistent teams in a group | When a room using those teams finishes a round |

- **Redis keys** (cache, rebuilt from Postgres on boot and nightly):
  `lb:world:round:{roundId}` · `lb:world:day:{dayKey}` · `lb:world:week:{weekKey}` · `lb:room:{roomId}:players` · `lb:room:{roomId}:teams` · `lb:group:{groupId}:week:{weekKey}` · `lb:group:{groupId}:teams:{weekKey}`.
  Round boards use one sortable number: `score × 100000 + (99999 − elapsed_ms)`, so the faster freeze wins ties (§5.7) without a second lookup.
- **Best-N boards:** per-user `lb:u:{userId}:day:{dayKey}` keeps that user's top 10 round scores (top 20 for week); on each new score the server recomputes the sum and writes it to the day/week board, so "best 10 / best 20" holds live.
- **Friends board:** read the friend ids' entries from the global board with one ZMSCORE call; nothing duplicated.
- **Push:** `leaderboard.update {board, top10, you:{rank, score}, teams?}` over WebSocket, at most once per second per board per client; Redis pub/sub fans out across server instances.
- **Reconciliation:** a job compares each live Redis board's top 100 against the Postgres views (`lb_world_day`, `lb_world_week`, `lb_team_round`, `lb_team_room`, `lb_team_week`) every 5 minutes and rebuilds any board that differs; mismatches are logged as incidents.

### 20.4 Group Room status presence (what other players see during a round)
Per player: `answering` (frozen), `locked in` (+ points), `missed` (wrong/timeout/unfreeze), `out` (2 failed freezes). Answer text is never shown to others before RESULTS.

## 21. Backend: storage, services, and data handling (v1.1)

### 21.1 Services
| Service | Role |
|---|---|
| API (Node.js + TypeScript, REST) | Accounts, profiles, groups, teams, packs, rooms, leaderboard reads, uploads |
| Game server (WebSocket) | Round clock, tile pushes, freezes, answer judging, live boards |
| Media worker (queue-driven) | Safety scan → normalize → tile → publish |
| Scheduler | World Live cycles, Daily set at 00:00 CT, weekly/day board rollover, reconciliation, purge jobs |
| PostgreSQL | System of record (`backend/schema.sql`) |
| Redis | Live boards, round state, pub/sub, rate limits |
| Object storage + CDN | Originals, tiles, avatars; signed URLs only |

### 21.2 Image storage pipeline
1. Client asks the API for an upload slot → presigned PUT to the **quarantine** bucket (15 MB cap, image types only).
2. Worker: hash-match against known child-abuse image lists, nudity/violence classifier. Fail → `media_assets.status = 'blocked'`, file kept only as the law requires, uploader notified.
3. Pass → strip EXIF/GPS, **fit the whole photo into a square (v1.1.2: no cropping; empty edges filled with a blurred, darkened copy of the photo)** unless the uploader chose a crop, resize to 1080 px, write `originals/{assetId}.jpg`.
4. Cut tiles for every grid size (4 + 9 + 16 + 25 + 36 + 64 + 100 = 254 tiles per photo) to `tiles/{photoId}/{N}/{index}.jpg`. Scrambled rounds serve the same tiles to different cells; rotation is applied by a signed image-transform parameter so the client never sees the source index.
5. `status = 'clean'`, photo becomes playable.

Buckets: `quarantine` (24 h lifecycle), `originals` (private), `tiles` (private, CDN with 60 s signed URLs), `avatars` (private, signed). No bucket is public.

### 21.3 REST API (v1)
```
GET    /v1/me                         POST /v1/me             DELETE /v1/me

Authorization is enforced in the API: group owner/admin manage teams and settings; pack owner edits packs; room host controls start/settings; everyone else reads what their membership allows. All writes are idempotent by client-supplied `Idempotency-Key` (24 h).

**WebSocket additions:** `room.presence {userId, team, status}` · `leaderboard.update {...}` · `team.update {teamId, roundScore, total}`. Client → server: `room.team {team}`.

### 21.4 Integrity and audit
- Scores are append-only (triggers block UPDATE/DELETE) and every score is appended to `score_ledger`, a SHA-256 hash chain. `ledger_verify()` returns the first broken sequence number or NULL; run it nightly and after every restore. Corrections are new compensating entries, never edits.
- Each round stores `sha256(seed)`; the seed is published after RESULTS so anyone can verify the tile order and scramble.
- Database checks reject a score above the §5.6 bound for that round's grid and scramble, a third freeze, a score whose mode differs from its round, and scramble/hint/small grids in ranked modes (World Live: Scrambled must be scramble level 2 and is otherwise held to the same rules).

### 21.5 Retention, backups, deletion
| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Scores + ledger | Indefinitely (pseudonymous after account deletion) |
| Attempts (answer text, flags) | 13 months |
| Quarantine uploads | 24 h |
| Deleted photos / packs | Removed from storage and tiles within 24 h; gone from backups within 35 days |
| Postgres backups | Point-in-time recovery 7 days; daily snapshots 35 days |
| Redis | Not backed up (rebuilt from Postgres) |

### 21.6 Capacity targets (launch)
10,000 concurrent players per region in World Live; 2,000 concurrent Group Rooms of up to 50; leaderboard push p95 < 1 s; freeze timestamp error < 50 ms (M3).

### 21.7 Acceptance tests (add to Section 18)
13. Two teams of 2 and 3 players: team round score equals the integer half-up average including players with no score (`lb_team_round`).
14. A player cannot be on two active teams in one group; leaving and joining another keeps both history rows.
15. Weekly board sums only the best 20 rounds; Monday 00:00 CT starts a new `week_key`; 2027-01-01 maps to 2026-W53.
16. Changing a stored score or ledger row is detected by `ledger_verify()` at the exact sequence number.
17. A live room board shows a player's "locked in" status to others within 1 s; answer text is not in any message before RESULTS.
18. Killing a Redis node and restarting rebuilds every board to match the Postgres views.
